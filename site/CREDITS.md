# Abstract Radio — music and visual credits
The site embeds the official YouTube player. Music remains hosted by YouTube and belongs to its respective creators and rights holders. Featured song buttons point to YouTube videos; visitors can also paste a YouTube or YouTube Music video link. Availability and embedding permissions depend on the source video.
The player interface and abstract song icons were created for this project. Google Fonts supplies DM Sans and Manrope under the SIL Open Font License; the page falls back to system fonts.
