# After Hours — audio and artwork
Blue Hour, Soft Focus, and Night Garden are original procedural instrumental sketches generated by this project's JavaScript using sine waves and seeded noise. Each lasts 32 seconds. No recordings, samples, third-party songs, or external audio services are used.
The abstract SVG album covers were also created for this project.
